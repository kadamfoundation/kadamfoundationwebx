# Kadam Foundation: One Step That Travelled Miles

_The story of how it all began — 20 March 2020_

On 20 March 2020, I witnessed something from my terrace that would change the direction of my life.

Near my home, I saw a few children searching for food in a dustbin. I could not simply stand there and watch.

I went downstairs and approached them. When I asked what had happened, their response stayed with me:

**“Didi, bhookh lagi hai. Humne kuch nahi khaya.”**

_“Sister, we are hungry. We haven't eaten anything.”_

I returned home and brought them food supplies from my own house. It was a small gesture, but as I spoke with the children, I asked another question:

_“Tum school jaate ho?”_ — Do you go to school?

Their answer was simple: _“Hum school nahi jaate.”_ — We don't go to school.

In that moment, I realised that hunger was not the only challenge these children faced. Some were also missing out on something fundamental: access to education.

I was a schoolgirl myself. I had no organisation, no team, no funding, and no roadmap for addressing these challenges. I did not know how far a small effort could go. But I knew I could not let that encounter become just another memory.

**On 21 March 2020, Kadam began.**

## A classroom in our veranda

We started _Barande Wali Kaksha_ — a classroom in our veranda.

There was no grand inauguration, sophisticated infrastructure, or elaborate plan. There was simply a small space, a few children, and a belief that every child deserved an opportunity to learn.

We began with just **10 children**.

Gradually, more children joined. One child brought another, and our little veranda became a place of learning, friendship, and possibility. Over the next two years, the initiative grew into a learning centre that reached approximately 50–100 people.

For me, that veranda represented something important: we did not have to wait for perfect resources or ideal circumstances to begin making a difference.

We could start with what we had, where we were.

## When one classroom was no longer enough

In 2022, I had to move away from the place where it had all begun.

Leaving that veranda behind was difficult. But it also left me with a question that would shape Kadam's next chapter:

**What about the children in other slums? What about those we had not yet reached?**

I no longer wanted our efforts to depend solely on the children who could come to my veranda. I wanted to take the classroom to more communities — to places where children needed opportunities but did not necessarily have access to them.

That was when Kadam began to grow beyond a physical space.

What had started with a few children gradually developed into a WhatsApp group, then a wider community. Social media helped us connect with more people across Bhopal. Volunteers joined, ideas emerged, and individuals who shared our purpose began taking responsibility and creating opportunities in their own ways.

Over time, Kadam expanded its presence beyond Bhopal, reaching more than 15 cities.

What had begun as one small classroom was becoming a collective effort — sustained not by one person's actions alone, but by people choosing to contribute their time, skills, and commitment.

## One step, many possibilities

Sometimes, I look back at the girl standing on her terrace on 20 March 2020.

She had no idea what that moment would set in motion. She was not thinking about building an organisation across cities or bringing together a growing community of volunteers. She was simply unable to ignore the hunger and unmet needs of children living near her home.

She took one step.

And that step travelled miles.

Today, Kadam Foundation represents more than the children who once sat in our veranda. It represents every volunteer who chooses to serve, every young person who chooses to lead, every community that comes together, and every child who deserves an opportunity to learn and grow.

I started Kadam because I wanted to help a few children. I continue building it because I know there are many more who deserve to be reached.

I did not begin with a vision of how far we would go. I began by asking myself a simpler question:

**What can I do, right where I am?**

Perhaps that is what Kadam will always mean to me.

You do not have to change the whole world in a day.

Sometimes, all it takes is the courage to take **one Kadam**.

_— Prachi Dwivedi_  
_Founder & Director, Kadam Foundation_

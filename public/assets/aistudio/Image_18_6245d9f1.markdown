## Brand & Style

This design system embraces an authentic, human-centered NGO aesthetic designed for grassroots storytelling, credible youth leadership, and empathetic community mobilization. Rather than relying on cold corporate conventions or sterile SaaS-inspired white canvases, it establishes a grounded tactile warmth rooted in terracotta, sun-baked clay, soft peach, and deep espresso-ink tones.

### Design Movement & Character
- **Tactile Editorial NGO:** Inspired by print journalism, community chronicles, and tactile paper stock. Rich editorial serifs command headlines, paired with rhythmic, generous margins and soft, distinct program color planes.
- **Organic Earthiness:** Surfaces avoid pure digital white `#FFFFFF` in favor of baked peach canvas `#F2E3DC` and warm raised parchment `#F8EEE8`, creating an inviting, low-fatigue visual environment.
- **Youthful Authority:** Confident, deep ink actions and crisp typography counter-balance the pastel warmth, ensuring the UI remains grounded, accountable, and unmistakably authentic.

## Colors

The color system operates on an intentionally warm, single-mode palette reminiscent of earth, paper, and community craftsmanship. Dark mode is intentionally bypassed to maintain consistent physical print credibility and high-contrast warmth.

### Semantic Hierarchy & Roles
- **Primary Ink (`#3F2C2E`):** Anchors all core typography, primary button fills, iconography, and dominant navigation anchors. This deep brown-ink provides accessible contrast without the clinical starkness of pure black.
- **Secondary Brand Clay (`#8F6E64`) & Rose (`#C09E94`):** Provide mid-tier depth for narrative section headers, impact blocks, and soft organic breaks.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F2E3DC`):** The standard page canvas backdrop. All root containers sit atop this warm, dusty tint.
- **Raised Panels (`#F8EEE8`):** Applied to cards, quote panels, and elevated editorial story boxes to establish a soft paper layering effect.
- **Program Tint Surfaces:** Individual thematic cards use soft, desaturated historical tints:
  - `surface-clay-soft` (`#E6C8B8`): Community & Women Empowerment
  - `surface-blue-soft` (`#CDD0D2`): Youth Welfare & Leadership
  - `surface-olive-soft` (`#D0CCB1`): Environment & Ecology
  - `surface-lilac-soft` (`#D8C2D0`): Animal Welfare & Health

## Typography

The typographic pairing balances literary editorial sophistication with high-clarity transactional readability.

### Type Pairings
- **Display & Section Titles (`Lora`):** A contemporary serif font with brushed curves and sturdy letterforms. Communicates warmth, heritage, journalistic authenticity, and community voice.
- **Body, Controls & Metadata (`Inter`):** A neutral, highly legible sans-serif providing crystal-clear legibility across dense project reports, financial accountability figures, and form fields.

### Typographic Rules
- **Line Length:** Restrict reading containers to 45–75 characters (`max-width: 720px`) for continuous narrative copy.
- **Eyebrows:** Always set in `Inter`, uppercase, semibold, with `0.12em` tracking to cleanly introduce topics above `Lora` titles.
- **Numerics & Impact Metrics:** Hero statistics use large `Lora` numerals paired with `body-sm` sans-serif captions directly underneath.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a structured 4px linear rhythm built around an adaptable 12-column fluid grid system.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Grid Rules
- **Mobile (< 640px):** 4 columns, `gutter-mobile` (16px), `margin-mobile` (24px). Single card stacking.
- **Tablet (640px – 1023px):** 8 columns, `gutter-tablet` (20px), `margin-tablet` (32px). Two-column card grids.
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12 columns, `gutter` (24px), `margin` (40px to 56px). Max content container constrained to `1280px`.

### Editorial Spacing Rhythm
Vertical cadence emphasizes breathability and visual pauses between distinct storytelling milestones. Section paddings step cleanly: 48px on mobile, 64px on tablet, and 80px–96px on desktop showcases.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed primarily via **tonal layering** and **tactile borders** rather than prominent drop shadows or modern synthetic blurs.

### Tonal Stacking
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** `#F2E3DC` core page ground.
- **Level 1 (Panels & Story Cards):** `#F8EEE8` (light raised) or specific program tinted pastel planes (`#E6C8B8`, `#CDD0D2`, `#D0CCB1`, `#D8C2D0`).
- **Level 2 (Deep Bands & Callouts):** `#8F6E64` (brand clay) or `#E5D0C5` (warm header bands).

### Shadow Behavior
- **Base Components:** Flat with a subtle `1px solid #D4B9AE` bounding outline. Never combine heavy drop shadows with borders.
- **Occasional Lift (`shadow-sm`):** `0 2px 8px rgba(63, 44, 46, 0.06)` applied strictly to interactive cards or floating search inputs on hover.
- **Modals & Overlays (`shadow-md`):** `0 8px 24px rgba(63, 44, 46, 0.10)` on light tinted backdrop surfaces.

## Shapes

The shape philosophy pairs inviting organic card corners with fully rounded pill actions, echoing the curved warmth of the brand's footprint motif.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Buttons, Badges & Chips:** Fully pill-shaped (`border-radius: 999px`).
- **Cards, Panels & Feature Containers:** Smooth, prominent rounding between `18px` and `22px` (`radius-lg` / `rounded-xl`).
- **Form Controls & Inputs:** Soft, approachable `8px` (`radius-sm`).
- **Dividers & Strokes:** Soft 1px stroke weight using `border-subtle` (`#D4B9AE`).

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary CTA:**
  - Background: Brand Ink `#3F2C2E`, text: `#FFF7F2`, radius: `999px` (pill).
  - Height: `48px`, horizontal padding: `24px`.
  - Hover state: Darkens to `#2F2022` with a subtle `2px` forward shift on directional arrow icons.
- **Secondary CTA:**
  - Background: Transparent or `#F2E3DC`, border: `1px solid #3F2C2E`, text: `#3F2C2E`.
  - Radius: `999px`.
- **Keyboard Focus:** Visible `2px` offset ring in `#65413D` (`focus-ring`).

### Story & Program Cards
- **Structure:** `18px–22px` corner radius with `1px solid #D4B9AE` border.
- **Color Theming:** Color-coded according to the 4 focus sectors using the designated pastel surfaces (`surface-clay-soft`, `surface-blue-soft`, `surface-olive-soft`, `surface-lilac-soft`).
- **Typography:** Lora `headline-lg` titles accompanied by Inter `body-md` copy and `eyebrow` categorization tags.

### Form Inputs & Fields
- **Container:** `8px` radius, background `#F8EEE8`, border `1px solid #D4B9AE`.
- **States:** Active/focus shifts border to `#3F2C2E` with a subtle `#65413D` outer glow.
- **Labels:** Inter `label-md` in `#3F2C2E`, with error messages rendered strictly in `#8A3034`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Pill Shape:** Height `28px–32px`, horizontal padding `12px–16px`, radius `999px`.
- **Appearance:** Tinted program surface with `#3F2C2E` text for category tagging.

### Brand Mark Integration
- Incorporates the official circular footprint emblem asset with preserved warm clay background tone. When positioned alongside typography, maintain minimum breathing room equal to half the logo mark's width.
